3. Key corrections (common mistakes in name-based tables)

  • ZL111 = 354.0 (close), not AlSi8Cu3 / EN AC-46200. By composition (Si9/Cu1.5/Mg0.5) it maps to 354.0; name-guessed tables that write AlSi8Cu3 (Cu~3%, no Mg requirement) are wrong.
  • ZL205A has no Western equivalent — Cd+V+Zr+B+Ti micro-alloyed ultra-high-strength Al-Cu, China/aerospace proprietary. At most "same 206/A206 Al-Cu family, but not equivalent".
  • ZM6 ≈ EZ33A-type analog (Nd system, low Zn) — not ZE41A/RZ5 (which is high-Zn, Ce system — the opposite). A true 1:1 is Russian ML10.
  • ZM5 = AZ91C by industry convention, but its mean composition sits nearer AZ81A (lower Zn). AZ80 is a wrought alloy — don't use it for castings.
  • ZL104: by composition EN AC-43300 is closer than A360 (A360 is a high-pressure die-casting alloy with higher Fe).
